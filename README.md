# Affirmations

A simple and uplifting web app for generating positive affirmations to brighten your day. Whether you’re looking for a boost of confidence, motivation, or just a gentle reminder that you’re awesome, this app has you covered!

Get your daily affirmation here: https://cassianebel.github.io/affirmations/

## Features

- Generate Affirmations: Get a new positive affirmation with every click of a button.
- Customizable Aesthetic: Adjust the lightness/darkness of the background colors and choose your font to fit your vibe.
